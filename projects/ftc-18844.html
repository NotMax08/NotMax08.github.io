What it is

Competitive robotics with team 18844, ending at the 2025 FIRST World Championships. I was co-captain and owned the software side, which meant everything from the autonomous routines to the driver-assist code used during matches.

Localization

Autonomous scoring is entirely dependent on the robot knowing where it is. I built two-wheel odometry using dead wheels for translation, fused with IMU data for heading.

Where the constraint was

Dead wheel encoders are accurate over short distances but their heading estimate drifts badly across a full match, because small wheel slip integrates into a large angular error. The IMU has the opposite profile, its heading is stable but it is useless for translation.

So each sensor was used only for what it is actually good at. Heading from the IMU, translation from the encoders. That split is what kept the autonomous routines repeatable late in a match rather than only in the first thirty seconds.

Movement and pickup

On top of localization sat a point to point PID movement system, letting the autonomous routines be written as a sequence of field coordinates rather than timed motor commands. I also trained a custom CNN to drive an automated pickup system, so the robot could identify and collect game elements without precise driver alignment.

Result

1st place Connect Award, Ochoa Division, at the 2025 FIRST World Championships.
