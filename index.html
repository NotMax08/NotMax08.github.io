I build robots that learn how to move.

Mechatronics Engineering at the University of Waterloo. I work in high fidelity simulators, mostly NVIDIA Isaac Sim and Isaac Lab, using ROS2 for communication and a mix of reinforcement learning and classical control. Right now I am focused on quadruped locomotion policies and what it takes to get them off the simulator and onto real hardware.
