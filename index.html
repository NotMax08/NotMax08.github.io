The Brackey Way

Autonomous VLA sandwich assembly on Bracket Bot · team of four

A dual-arm Bracket Bot that takes toast out of a toaster, puts it on a plate, adds lettuce, and places the top slice. A fine-tuned π₀ VLA handles the difficult grasps, OpenCV + inverse kinematics handles the simple ones, and a state machine switches between them.

  • π₀ VLA
  • SFT / imitation learning
  • OpenCV (HSV)
  • inverse kinematics
  • state machine
  • Meta Quest teleop
Case study

highlights

  • Fine-tuned a π₀ vision-language-action model with supervised fine-tuning on 100+ teleoperated demonstrations (camera video and a text prompt in; joint positions and velocities out).
  • Resampled 65 Hz joint data and 5 fps camera frames onto one fixed time grid so every frame is paired with the right actions.
  • Varied object placement and lighting between demonstrations, and used DAgger-style data aggregation, to reduce overfitting.
  • Found the lettuce with HSV threshold segmentation and reached it with inverse kinematics, with wrist-camera closed-loop correction for 1–2 cm of head-camera calibration error.
  • Built a finite state machine that switches between the classical (CV + IK) and learned (VLA) controllers, advancing once the arm settles back at its home pose.

Aug – Sept 2026 robot learning in progress

Quadruped locomotion with PPO

Unitree Go2 · NVIDIA Isaac Lab · solo project

A walking policy for a Unitree Go2, trained from scratch with PPO in a custom manager-based RL environment I built in Isaac Lab.

  • Isaac Lab
  • PPO
  • rsl_rl
  • PyTorch
  • curriculum learning
Case study

highlights

  • Built a custom ManagerBasedRLEnv for flat-terrain Go2 locomotion: 48-dim observations, 12-DOF joint-position actions, and a custom reward stack.
  • Designed a sigmoid curriculum that ramps up the foot-lift reward over training, so the policy learns to balance before the reward starts shaping its gait.
  • Early policies learned to crawl; a custom height reward fixed it, and squared action-rate penalties keep joint motion smooth.
  • Next: get-up recovery policies and domain randomization for sim-to-real transfer.

May – July 2026 autonomy

Autonomous maze navigation

NVIDIA Nova Carter · Isaac Sim 5.1 · ROS2 · solo project

A ROS2 navigation stack on a Nova Carter in Isaac Sim. The robot drives into a maze it hasn't seen, builds a map with SLAM, localizes with AMCL, and uses Nav2 to plan and follow a path to the goal.

  • ROS2
  • Nav2
  • slam_toolbox
  • AMCL
  • Isaac Sim
  • RViz
Case study

highlights

  • Integrated slam_toolbox mapping, AMCL localization, and Nav2 with a Regulated Pure Pursuit controller, debugging in RViz.
  • Built the full TF tree (map → odom → chassis_link → sensors) with Isaac Sim action graphs; debugged QoS mismatches, sensor-rate bottlenecks, and frame-authority conflicts.
  • Traced a warped SLAM map to LiDAR scans lagging odometry during turns, and fixed it by raising the RTX LiDAR scan rate to 20 Hz.

Eureka Hacks vision & manipulation

RICO, a tool-fetching arm

4-DOF arm · overhead camera · hackathon team

Ask for a tool in plain language and the arm finds it on the bench and hands it to you. A Groq LLM plans the steps, YOLOv8 finds the tool, and inverse kinematics moves the arm.

  • YOLOv8
  • OpenCV homography
  • inverse kinematics
  • Groq LLM
  • Python
Case study

highlights

  • A Groq LLM turns a plain-language request into a list of robot actions.
  • A homography maps YOLOv8 detections from pixels to positions on the bench, so tools can go anywhere instead of at fixed spots.
  • An inverse kinematics solver turns that position into the joint angles needed to reach and grab the tool.

Case study

highlights

  • Fused two-wheel odometry with IMU heading, rotating robot-frame motion into field coordinates.
  • Automated sample pickup with OpenCV and a custom-trained CNN that estimates each sample's distance and orientation.
