About

I like robots that have to deal with the real world.

I'm a Mechatronics Engineering student at the University of Waterloo (Honours Co-op, class of 2031). I'm most interested in robots that work in the physical world: legged locomotion, mobile autonomy, and manipulation that mixes learned policies with classical control.

Most of my recent work is robot learning. On WATonomous's humanoid team I fine-tuned π₀.5 for pick and place and wrote the leader-arm recorder that collects its demonstrations. At the BOTS hackathon my team fine-tuned π₀ to pull toast out of a toaster, which won 1st place. On my own I'm training a Unitree Go2 to walk with PPO in Isaac Lab, and I built a ROS2 Nav2 stack that maps and navigates a maze in Isaac Sim.

Before university I was co-captain and lead programmer of FTC team 18844. We represented Team Canada at the 2025 FIRST World Championships, and I ran robot showcases and SPIKE robotics workshops for 60+ kids across the GTA.
