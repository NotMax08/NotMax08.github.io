About

I study Mechatronics Engineering at the University of Waterloo. On the WATonomous humanoid team I fine-tune π₀.5, a vision-language-action model, so the robot's left arm can pick up a cube and place it in a tray in Isaac Sim.

Before that I was co-captain and lead programmer of FTC team 18844, Team Canada at FIRST Worlds 2025. I also build robots at hackathons: a dual-arm robot that makes a sandwich won 1st place at BOTS.

I'm interested in robot learning, VLAs, world action models and reinforcement learning, and I'm open to robotics and ML co-op roles. or read my résumé.
